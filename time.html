Rainbow Doggy's Time Guide

Woof! I'm Rainbow Doggy, and I'm SO excited to show you around the clock! These are my best tips — let's sniff them out together! 🌈🐶

  • Rainbow Zoom ⚡ (the minute hand): That's my speedy best friend! Rainbow Zoom zooms all the way around the track — 60 steps — every single hour. Zoom zoom! 🐾
  • Rainbow Float 🌈 (the hour hand): Rainbow Float is like me on a leisurely walkies — she takes her sweet time! She floats slowly between the numbers for the whole time Rainbow Zoom is zooming the lap. She only moves to the next number once Rainbow Zoom zooms all the way round!
  • O'Clock Sharp (Start of the Hour): When Rainbow Zoom points straight up at the 12 — a brand new hour starts! We both go "WOOF!" and the minutes display resets to exactly 00. Fresh start!
  • The Quarter-Pizza Slices (Fractions 15, 30, 45): Imagine we're sharing a pizza! At 15 minutes we've eaten one quarter (1/4) — that's quarter past! At 30 minutes we've eaten half (1/2) — that's half past! At 45 minutes there's just one quarter slice left to go — that's quarter to!
  • The "PAST" Zone (Minutes 1 to 30): The green side — this is where Rainbow Zoom runs away from the last hour! We look back and say how many minutes have gone PAST. It's like counting how many steps I've run since the park gate!
  • The "TO" Zone (Minutes 31 to 59): The blue side — now Rainbow Zoom is racing towards the next hour! We count down how many steps are left TO go. At 8:40, there are 20 steps still TO reach 9 o'clock — nearly there!
  • The Rainbow Float Trick! 🐾 At 8:50, Rainbow Float is almost at the 9 — so close it looks like 9 o'clock! But the hour only changes when Rainbow Zoom finishes the whole lap and gets back to 12. Until then, it's still 8! Don't be fooled by how close Rainbow Float looks!
