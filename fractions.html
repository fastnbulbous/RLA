🍕 What IS a Fraction?

A fraction means some things out of a whole group — like 3 out of 8 pizza slices, or 3 out of 8 blankets! 🐾

3
8
← 3 — parts we are talking about
← 8 — total pieces in the group
So: 3 out of 8! 🎉

🧠 The Sneaky Trap!

Bigger bottom number = SMALLER pieces! A treat cut into 8 pieces gives smaller pieces than one cut into 4. So 1/8 < 1/4!

⚖️ Comparing Fractions

  • Same denominator: Compare numerators! 3/8 > 1/8
  • Same numerator: Bigger denom = smaller fraction! 1/3 > 1/5
  • Use ½ benchmark: If top is > half of bottom, the fraction is >½
  • Cross multiply: 3/4 vs 5/7 → 3×7=21 vs 5×4=20 → 3/4 > 5/7

🔗 Shared Bottom Numbers (LCD)

To compare fractions with different bottoms, find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCD) — the smallest number both denominators go into.

Example: 1/4 + 1/6
Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12…
Multiples of 6: 6, 12…
LCD = 12 → 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12 ✓

♻️ Same-size Fractions

Same amount, different numbers! Multiply top AND bottom by the same number:

1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8 = 8/16

🔍 GCD — Greatest Common Divisor

The GCD is the biggest number that divides evenly into both. Use it to simplify fractions!

Simplify 6/8:
Factors of 6: 1, 2, 3, 6
Factors of 8: 1, 2, 4, 8
GCD = 2 → 6÷2=3, 8÷2=4 → 3/4 ✓
